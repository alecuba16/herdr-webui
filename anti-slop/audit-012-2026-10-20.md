## Findings

None in the initial pass. A post-delivery re-read against the persisted
logs found four overstated or stale claims, all corrected in the follow-up
commit:

1. LOW (C-5, self-contradiction): the doc called the earlier "+61px
   transient" an instrumentation artifact, but the corrected harness
   reproduces exactly +61px settled (481 to 542) for the first expand at
   3000. Only the early measurement method and the transient
   interpretation were wrong, not the figure. Reworded.
2. MEDIUM (C-5, overstated attribution): "first expand shifts 1147px
   because the two-render sequence doubles the wipe churn" attributed the
   421px difference to the second wipe alone. The passes sit at
   different scrollTop (6252 vs 6500) with different click baselines
   (255 vs 482, mid-realization), so the original logs cannot separate
   the second wipe from the unstable baseline. A follow-up controlled
   re-run (settled baseline, deep-probe suite) still measures exactly
   +1147px, which settles the split: the baseline contributes zero and
   the first-expand pass mechanics carry the full extra ~421px; the
   doc cites that run. Reworded accordingly.
3. LOW (C-5, mechanism contradiction): "the cached expand at 3000
   re-renders with the same placeholder mix already settled, so the
   wipe is height-neutral" contradicted mechanism B: the render-time
   sample shows the wipe resetting the band (marker 1082px mid-pass)
   before reconverging to 542px. Reworded to reset-then-reconverge.
4. LOW (C-5, stale state): "currently untracked" harness claims went
   stale when the scripts were committed in the same commit; also the
   "renders a loading state" phrase overstated what the first expand
   render emits (no renderer reads `state.loading`, only the caret icon
   changes), and the "flash" wording claimed paint for mid-wipe frames
   that never yield to the event loop (the cached pass restores
   scrollTop in the same task; the post-restore slide is the visible
   part). All reworded; the contiguity of the 122-row placeholder band
   beyond the 30 stored sample indices is now labeled inferred.

Drafting-time corrections (kept for the record): the 3000 cached delta
was first written as +60px from stale console output (persisted
evidence: 0); the 1082 to 542 move was first attributed to the 6500 run
(it belongs to 3000); the 6/15 child-height split behind 726px was
arithmetic-consistent but not directly evidenced, softened to the sum
matching.
